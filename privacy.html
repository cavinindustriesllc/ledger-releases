Privacy policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · Applies to the Ledger app for iPhone, the Ledger web app and the Ledger desktop app.

The short version

We don’t collect any data (in App Store terms: Data Not Collected). Everything you enter in Ledger stays on your device. There is no account to create, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Ledger never sends your books to us or to anyone else. It connects to a bank only if you set that up yourself on the desktop app (see below).

Where your information is stored

  • iPhone app: in Ledger’s private folder on your iPhone, protected by iOS Data Protection (encrypted while your phone is locked). If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, your iPhone backup includes this folder, under Apple’s terms for those backups.
  • Web app: in your browser’s storage on that device. Clearing the site’s data in your browser erases it.
  • Desktop app: in files in Ledger’s data folder on your computer.

We (the developer) can’t see, access or recover any of it.

What we collect

Nothing. Ledger contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or other third-party software that sends data off your device. The app makes no network requests with your information. The web app is downloaded from our web host like any website, and the host may keep standard technical logs of those requests (such as IP address and time) for security and reliability; those logs never contain your books.

Update checks (desktop app)

The desktop app checks for a newer version by downloading a small release file from our public releases page on GitHub. That request carries no information about you or your books; GitHub may keep standard technical logs of it under GitHub’s privacy statement. Ledger asks before installing any update.

Optional bank connection (desktop app)

On a computer, Ledger can import transactions from your bank, read-only, but only if you set up that connection yourself with your own Plaid account and your own small relay service. In that case your bank sign-in happens in Plaid’s own window under Plaid’s privacy policy, and your transaction data travels from Plaid through your relay to the Ledger app on your computer. We (Cavin Industries) are not part of that path and never receive it. If you don’t set this up, Ledger makes no bank connection at all; importing a statement file from your bank’s website is the standard way to bring in transactions.

Files you choose to share

When you export a book or a backup, Ledger creates a file and hands it to the place you pick (for example Files, AirDrop, Mail or a download). What happens to it after that is up to you and that service. Ledger deletes its own temporary copy right away.

Deleting your data

In the iPhone app and the web app, go to Settings → Delete all data to erase every book, backup and setting Ledger keeps on that device. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone. In the desktop app, remove books from the app or delete Ledger’s data folder.

Children

Ledger isn’t directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and its effective date. If Ledger ever starts to collect data (for example, for optional sync between devices), we’ll say so here and in the app before it happens.
