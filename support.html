Common questions

Where are my books saved?

Only on your device: in Ledger’s private folder on iPhone, in your browser for the web app, and in Ledger’s data folder on a computer. There’s no account and nothing is uploaded, so we can’t restore lost data for you.

How do I back up or move my books?

In Ledger, open Settings → Export a backup. On iPhone this opens the Share sheet, so you can save the file to Files or iCloud Drive or AirDrop it. To bring books back, use Settings → Restore from a backup file. Restoring adds books; it never replaces the ones already there.

Can I get an older version of a book?

Yes. Ledger keeps a daily copy of each book (the last 30) plus any you make with Back up now. Find them under Settings → Backups.

How do I import my bank statement?

Download a CSV file from your bank’s website, then open a personal ledger and choose Import CSV. On a computer, Ledger can also connect to a bank read-only if you set that up yourself (see the app’s Bank connections screen). To try it first, save this sample statement (made-up transactions) to Files and import it.

Are the tax figures on Owner’s desk tax advice?

No. They’re estimates calculated from the percentages you enter, to help you plan. Check with a tax professional.

How do I delete everything?

In the iPhone app or the web app, open Settings → Delete all data. It asks you to confirm, then erases every book, backup and setting on that device. It can’t be undone, so export a backup first if you might want your books later.
